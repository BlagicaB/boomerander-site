Questions

The short answers.

What is a Boomerander?

A Boomerander is a Michigander who moved away, built a life or a career somewhere else and came back to Michigan on purpose. The word is a play on Michigander.

How many people who leave Michigan move back?

About a third. The Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland found that 34% of young adults who leave metro Detroit eventually move back, compared with 30% for Grand Rapids and a 28% national average.

How many people leave Michigan each year?

About 155,000 people moved from Michigan to another state in 2023 and about 135,000 moved in, according to U.S. Census Bureau data. From July 2024 to July 2025 Michigan gained 1,796 more residents from other states than it lost, its first net gain since at least 1990.

What is the Brain Gain?

The Brain Gain is Boomerander's free network of Michigan people with experience from other places. Members use it to find jobs, hire, mentor, make introductions and help other people move back to Michigan.

Do I need to be looking for a job to join?

No. Plenty of Boomeranders already have jobs. You can join to mentor, make introductions, share your story or help someone else find their way home.

Is Boomerander a government program?

No. Boomerander is an independent community built by people who left Michigan and came back. It is not run by a state agency or an economic development group.

Who can join?

Anyone who moved back to Michigan, anyone planning to come back, Michiganders who never left but want to help and employers looking for people with outside experience.
